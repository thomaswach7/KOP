How laser cutting cost is calculated

No black-box pricing: the model is deliberately simple and follows the usual job-shop costing.

Material = (bounding rectangle + allowance) × thickness × density × price per kg
Cutting = (cut length ÷ feed rate + pierces × pierce time) × machine hourly rate
Setup = setup cost per item ÷ quantity
Unit price = (material + cutting + setup + bending + extras) × (1 + markup)

The bundled feed rates and material prices are starting values for a 4 kW fiber laser. Enter your own figures and the prices will be right. Nesting several parts on one sheet is not calculated.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Which DXF files are supported?

ASCII DXF from release R12 onwards with lines, arcs, circles, polylines, ellipses, splines and block references. Binary DXF and DWG are not read – save the drawing as ASCII DXF in your CAD system.

Are my drawings uploaded?

No. The file is analysed entirely in your browser. Your settings are stored locally on your computer as well.

How accurate is the calculation?

Cut length, pierces and area are computed exactly from the geometry. The price is as accurate as the figures behind it: tune feed rates, hourly rate and material prices to your shop once.

Does it work for plasma, waterjet or oxy-fuel too?

Yes. The model is process-independent: you enter the feed rate and pierce time of your machine per material and thickness.

Does it take nesting into account?

No. Material is charged by the smallest bounding rectangle of the part plus an allowance. That is common practice for quoting and errs on the safe side.

How do I get the Pro version?

After purchase you receive a license key that you paste into the app under “License”. The license is valid for twelve months.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start.
